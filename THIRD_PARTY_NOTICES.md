Source: https://en-word.net/ and https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet

Underlying Princeton WordNet material remains subject to its original license and disclaimer. Both license texts are included in `licenses/` and `public/licenses/`; the latter is packaged with the app.

QuickSense converts the official 2025 XML archive into lowercase, letter-indexed JSON. It groups senses by lemma, keeps up to 12 senses, up to two examples and eight synonyms per sense, and deduplicates definitions. This WordNet conversion adds no bilingual definitions or translation model; a separate ECDICT companion supplies Chinese definitions. The manifest records the source URL, archive SHA-256, conversion date and retained counts. These modifications are by the QuickSense project and do not imply endorsement by the source authors.
